How we operate

What the holding company brings to every subsidiary.

Governance and compliance

Each subsidiary keeps its own board and accountability, and answers to the group on a single set of standards: safeguarding, data protection, and honest reporting. Compliance is designed into each business, not added afterwards.

Capital

The group funds each business through its early stages and holds for the long term, so subsidiaries can build carefully rather than chase the next quarter. Capital follows a clear plan, not a headline.

Shared services

Finance, legal, software and brand sit at group level, so a new business starts with infrastructure already in place. CNote Solutions builds and maintains the group's own products, keeping engineering in-house.

Delivery standards

Every subsidiary is held to the same bar on accessibility, security and follow-through. Where we serve people at a hard moment or a public body under scrutiny, the work has to be right the first time.

Reach

Where the group operates.

The group operates in England and across three southern African countries. Our footprint grows as businesses move from development into operation.
